Dish Pong

A small network of simulated neurons learns Pong the way Cortical Labs’ CL1 brain cells do. Nobody tells it what to do. A hit sends back a calm, steady beat. A miss sends back a burst of random noise. Watch it get better.

Inside the dish

Is it learning?

This is a simulation, not living cells. It borrows the idea behind Cortical Labs’ DishBrain and CL1 work: neurons settle into whatever behaviour makes their world predictable. Here, a hit makes the feedback predictable and strengthens the connections that were just active. A miss scrambles them a little, and a bigger miss scrambles them more.
